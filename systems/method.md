# Engagement Method: Strategic Brand Architecture

## Sequence
Listen → Locate → Clarify → Structure → Activate → Prove

## Client Problem Center
Founders arrive recognizing value, clarifying priorities, and determining what to build. The sequence carries that clarity into connected brand, platforms, and systems as one continuous engagement.

## Supporting Philosophical Frameworks
The frameworks in `/frameworks/` inform the discovery and calibration layers where appropriate. They are not required diagnostic tools. Framework-to-phase mappings remain proposed (INFERENCE) until confirmed.

## Outcome Scope
Current proof documents clarity, direction, and deployed infrastructure. Measured commercial outcomes (traffic, revenue, bookings) are not yet established. The "Prove" step distinguishes functionality, client feedback, and measured business outcomes to preserve future validation capacity.
