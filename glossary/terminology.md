# Glossary & Language Rules

## Canonical Terms
- **Strategic Brand Architecture** — The category. Discovery-to-build sequence.
- **Authentic Momentum** — Company and book name. Methodology naming remains open.
- **Founders and public figures** — Primary audience. "Clients" remains acceptable.
- **Prove** — Check the delivered work against the agreed purpose and success criteria, distinguishing functionality, client feedback, and measured business outcomes.

## Language to Avoid
- "Midas touch" or guaranteed scaling promises (contradicts cyclical growth model) [INFERENCE]
- "Hustle harder" or linear effort framing (opposes category reframe) [INFERENCE]
- Clinical or psychological diagnoses (observations are shared interpretations) [SUMMARY]

## Tone Markers [HYPOTHESIS]
- Observational, not prescriptive
- Structural and grounded
- Non-performative
