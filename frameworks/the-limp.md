# The Limp

## Definition
The pattern of giving without boundaries and rationalizing the deficit as faith or virtue. [VERBATIM]

## Components
Identification of non-reciprocal giving, Pythagoras question (lifting vs. laying down), 24-hour boundary rule.

## Function
Restoring energy balance and self-respect in service-oriented founders.

## What It Replaces
Virtue-driven overextension.

## Application Context [INFERENCE]
Proposed for use during Locate/Clarify when founders report burnout or resentment.
