# Both Torches

## Definition
A decision-validation model pairing intuition (resonance) with reason (testing). [VERBATIM]

## Components
- **Torch 1 (Intuition):** Expansion check, energy audit, future pull, body compass.
- **Torch 2 (Reason):** External verification, prediction tracking, evidence checking.

## Function
Weighing opportunities, relationships, or directions before committing.

## What It Replaces
Decision-making based solely on impulse or solely on external data.

## Application Context [INFERENCE]
Proposed for use in Clarify/Structure phases when founders face directional choices.
