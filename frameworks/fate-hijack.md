# The FATE Hijack

## Definition
Four unconscious pathways that drain energy and override internal knowing. [VERBATIM]

## Components
- **Focus:** Attention scattered by notifications, obligations, and urgencies that serve someone else.
- **Authority:** Overriding your own knowing because someone with a title or a louder voice says so.
- **Tribe:** Paying with your energy to fit in: laughing at jokes that aren't funny, staying quiet when you have something to say.
- **Emotion:** Fear, guilt, and shame used to steer your decisions.

## Function
Diagnostic tool for decision fatigue and automatic compliance.

## What It Replaces
The assumption that low output equals low capability.

## Application Context [INFERENCE]
Proposed for use during Locate/Clarify when founders report exhaustion or scattered direction.
