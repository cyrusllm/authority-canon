# Listen → Locate → Clarify → Structure → Activate → Prove

## Definition
A six-step sequence that moves founders from observation to deployment. Begins with attentive listening to words, reactions, enthusiasm, and hesitation. Observations become shared interpretations explored with the client. Clarity becomes direction. Direction becomes architecture. Architecture becomes build. Build becomes proof.

## Components
- **Listen:** Attentive observation of language, energy, and hesitation
- **Locate:** Identifying unrecognized strengths and scattered priorities
- **Clarify:** Shared interpretation and priority sequencing
- **Structure:** Translating direction into a structural blueprint
- **Activate:** Building the connected brand, platforms, and systems
- **Prove:** Check the delivered work against the agreed purpose and success criteria, distinguishing functionality, client feedback, and measured business outcomes. [APPROVED]

## Function
Replaces fragmented requests with a continuous discovery-to-build sequence.

## Application Context
Primary engagement structure for founders and public figures seeking clarity and connected infrastructure.

## What It Replaces
Transactional requests for isolated assets without directional alignment.
