## Approval Protocol
- DRAFT, INFERENCE, and HYPOTHESIS labels remain intact. Approval of v1.2 does not convert proposals into confirmed facts or required methods.
- "Prove" step definition is approved. Missing commercial metrics limit current claims, not the method's future capacity.
- Unverified source references are excluded from public copy until confirmed in `/sources/verification-log.md`.
